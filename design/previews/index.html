Nine ways to cast a ballot

Design studies for the election platform. Each one is the same sitting (Prime Minister, a referendum, a pick-two) played through from arrival to receipt, with sound. Three tones, three designs each, and the three gesture styles spread across them. Use the pill in the corner of a study to restart it, mute it, or come back here.

A · Solemn could pass for a real country's polling day

A1

The Booth

Behind a green curtain: a pencil on a string, three tinted papers to cross and fold, a sealed steel box.

draw and drag, or tap
A2

The Rotunda

Bronze doors, engraved cards, a fountain pen. One envelope closed with wax and dropped into a glass urn.

tap, then watch
A3

The Lever Machine

A mechanical voting machine in hammertone enamel. Slide the levers, throw the red master lever, the counter rolls.

gestures only

B · Serious craft, in-universe wit dignified, with Grandpa Clifford looking over your shoulder

B1

The Postal Kit

A postal vote at the kitchen table. Tear the strip, tick with a biro, tuck into "Nobody's Business", seal, post.

draw and drag, or tap
B2

The Punch Ticket

The ballot is a card ticket and the vote is a hole. Chads fall, the orange machine eats it. Holes cannot be undone, only reissued.

tap, then watch
B3

The Marble Poll

Painted drums in a sunlit yard. One glass marble, one ding of the bell, and a finger dipped in indelible ink.

drag, or tap twice

C · Full USFC bizarre the country of the bizarre votes accordingly

C1

The Hotline

The red telephone to the Bureau of Counting. Lift the receiver, dial your opinion on a rotary dial, hang up to send.

gestures only
C2

The Cliff

In the Free Cliffs you vote by throwing it off one. Paper planes into candidates' balloons, then toll the bell.

throw, or tap
C3

Democracy-O-Matic

A BuildCorp-sponsored arcade cabinet. Insert one citizen, mash the glowing buttons, collect your sticker.

tap, then watch
